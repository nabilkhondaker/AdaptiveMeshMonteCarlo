# Stochastic Models

Author: Nabil Khondaker

## GBM
Euler–Maruyama / exact log-Euler for geometric Brownian motion.

## Heston
Full truncation or reflection schemes for the variance CIR process; documented in source.

## Jump Diffusion
Merton lognormal jumps with compensator.

## Multi-Asset
Cholesky-driven correlated Brownian increments; correlation diagnostics included.
